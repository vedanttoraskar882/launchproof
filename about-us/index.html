About LaunchProof

Built for the decision behind your first stock order

Helping UK physical-product founders investigate demand, compare supplier costs and test customer response before committing capital.

Our Vision

“Our vision is to help physical-product founders make informed decisions before committing money to inventory by bringing demand, competition, pricing and supplier risk evidence into one structured validation process.”

The Commercial Problem

A product can attract clicks and encouraging feedback while remaining too expensive to source, fulfil or advertise. LaunchProof is being developed to bring customer evidence, pricing, supplier terms and acquisition costs into one Go, Revise or Stop recommendation. The founder makes the final decision.

Intended Customers

First-time physical-product founders.
Small and medium-sized ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands testing a new line.
Agencies, product developers and incubators supporting founders.

Initial UK Focus Categories

Health and wellness consumables Pet products Home and kitchen goods Personal accessories

Indicative development roadmap

Our planned development sequence as we build and refine LaunchProof with early-access founders.

1

Core platform development

Architecting the analytical engine, data pipeline models and initial validation scoring infrastructure.

2

Early-access feedback and pilot preparation

Working directly with early-access registrants to test usability, refine inputs and calibrate decision thresholds.

3

Pilot validation and refinement

Running initial live demand test pilots in selected UK physical-product categories with founder-controlled ad accounts.

4

Planned outcome tracking and integration expansion

Expanding long-term post-launch tracking with Shopify and Amazon integrations under Module 9 (Outcome Loop).
