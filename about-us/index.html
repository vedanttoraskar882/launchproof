About LaunchProof

Built for the decision behind your first stock order

Helping UK physical-product founders investigate demand, compare supplier costs and test customer response before committing capital.

The Commercial Problem

A product can attract clicks and encouraging feedback while remaining too expensive to source, fulfil or advertise. LaunchProof is designed to bring customer evidence, pricing, supplier terms and acquisition costs into one Go, Revise or Stop recommendation. The founder makes the final decision.

Intended Customers

First-time physical-product founders.
Small and medium-sized ecommerce and direct-to-consumer brands testing a new line.
Agencies, product developers and incubators supporting founders.

Initial UK Focus Categories

Health and wellness consumables Pet products Home and kitchen goods Personal accessories
