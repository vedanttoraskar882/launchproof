Innovation & Approach

What Makes LaunchProof Different?

A purposeful validation system designed to replace costly guesswork with structured commercial evidence.

Integrated product validation

LaunchProof is being developed to bring demand, competition, pricing and supplier risk analysis together in one product validation process.

Evidence-based insights

The planned platform will help founders assess evidence and make informed decisions before investing in inventory.

Structured decision framework

LaunchProof’s planned decision framework will combine multiple factors to assess product viability and support a Go, Revise or Stop recommendation.

Decision Standard

Clear commercial standards: Go, Revise or Stop

Designed to help founders evaluate customer response against pre-agreed thresholds to provide one of three unambiguous recommendations. The founder always retains final authority.

Go

Proceed to stock order

Customer evidence confirms willingness to pay at a price that leaves a viable margin, and projected demand justifies the supplier’s minimum order. Go leads to a supplier brief and staged spending plan.

Revise

Refine and retest

Customers respond well to the concept or price, but acquisition costs or the supplier’s minimum order quantity create excess initial risk. Revise identifies what to change and retest before ordering stock.

Stop

Preserve capital

Customer demand or unit economics fail to support placing an order under realistic marketing costs. Stop explains why the evidence does not support committing to stock, preserving capital for other opportunities.

Methodology Overview

How a LaunchProof validation works

A disciplined five-stage validation schedule designed to reach a confident commercial decision.

Target: Days 0–2

Define the idea & demand

Capture audience, budget and risk appetite. Research demand trends and review customer complaints.

Target: Days 2–4

Check product economics

Screen compliance requirements, compare supplier terms, and calculate landed costs before live testing.

Target: Days 4–5

Approve concept & rules

Approve specification and price range. Check claims, prepare test pages and lock decision thresholds.

Target: Days 5–12

Test demand at stated price

Live customer test via founder-owned advertising accounts with transparent waitlists and optional deposits.

Target: Days 12–14

Review evidence & decide

Review acquisition costs, evidence score and supplier risk to deliver the final recommendation.
