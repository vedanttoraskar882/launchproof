Methodology & Schedule

How a LaunchProof validation works

A disciplined five-stage validation process. Timings reflect the planned pilot schedule.

Days 0–2 Pilot Schedule

Define the idea and investigate demand

Capture the audience, budget and risk appetite. Identify assumptions, research demand and review recurring customer complaints.

Days 2–4 Pilot Schedule

Check the product economics

Screen category requirements, compare competitor prices and supplier terms, and calculate landed cost. Advance to a live test only if there is a viable price range.

Days 4–5 Pilot Schedule

Approve the concept and lock the rules

The founder approves the specification and price range. Check proposed claims, prepare test pages and agree the Go, Revise and Stop thresholds before advertising spend begins.

Days 5–12 Pilot Schedule

Test demand at a stated price

Explain to visitors that the product is in development. Compare price-visible waitlist responses and optional refundable deposits within the founder’s approved advertising budget.

Days 12–14 Pilot Schedule

Review the evidence and decide

Review acquisition cost, the score range and supplier order risk. Go leads to a supplier brief and staged spending plan. Revise identifies what to change and retest. Stop explains why the evidence does not support committing to the order.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity on our evidence methodology, test mechanics, and pilot expectations.

What can I learn for free?

Free Discovery covers one idea per month, with basic Market Radar, a competitor price map, the top three complaint themes and an initial compliance category flag.

What counts as evidence that customers will buy?

LaunchProof gives greater weight to actions taken after a visitor sees the price. An optional refundable deposit is a stronger signal than a positive comment. The score is shown as a range to reflect uncertainty. Modelled evidence alone cannot trigger a Go recommendation.

Why might a promising idea receive a Revise recommendation?

Customers may respond well to the advertised price while acquisition costs or a supplier’s minimum order make the first batch uneconomic. Revise identifies a specific change to test, such as a smaller pack size or a different supplier.

Who pays for advertising and handles deposits?

The founder pays the advertising platform directly. Optional refundable deposits go through the founder’s own payment account. LaunchProof does not hold the advertising budget or customer deposits.

Does the compliance pre-screen certify my product?

No. It flags likely requirements, indicative costs and lead times, and points founders towards relevant guidance, advisers or test houses. Categories with heavy pre-market requirements, such as medical devices, can be flagged but are outside active launch support.

What do I receive after a full validation?

A Go, Revise or Stop recommendation with reasons, a Validation Evidence Score range and a Signed Evidence Pack. Where the evidence supports proceeding, the handoff includes a supplier brief and a Capital-at-Risk Ladder showing when to commit the next portion of the budget.

Can I validate one idea without a subscription?

Yes. The planned Validation Sprint is £149 per sprint, excluding VAT. Expert Review is a separate £450 per review, excluding VAT.
