Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity on our evidence methodology, planned test mechanics and early-access pilot scope.

What can I learn for free?

Free Discovery is planned to cover one idea per month, with basic Market Radar, a competitor price map, the top three complaint themes and an initial compliance category flag.

What counts as evidence that customers will buy?

LaunchProof is designed to give greater weight to actions taken after a visitor sees the price. An optional refundable deposit is a stronger signal than a positive comment. The score is shown as a range to reflect uncertainty. Modelled evidence alone cannot trigger a Go recommendation.

Why might a promising idea receive a Revise recommendation?

Customers may respond well to the advertised price while acquisition costs or a supplier’s minimum order make the first batch uneconomic. Revise identifies a specific change to test, such as a smaller pack size or a different supplier.

Who pays for advertising and handles deposits?

Under our planned commercial model, the founder pays the advertising platform directly. Optional refundable deposits go through the founder’s own payment account. LaunchProof does not hold the advertising budget or customer deposits.

Does the compliance pre-screen certify my product?

No. It flags likely requirements, indicative costs and lead times, and points founders towards relevant guidance, advisers or test houses. Categories with heavy pre-market requirements, such as medical devices, can be flagged but are outside active launch support.

What do I receive after a full validation?

A Signed Evidence Pack documenting test results, demand metrics, supplier economics and a transparent Go, Revise or Stop decision dossier with staged spend recommendations.

Can I validate one idea without a subscription?

Yes. The proposed Validation Sprint offers a standalone validation engagement without requiring an active monthly subscription.
